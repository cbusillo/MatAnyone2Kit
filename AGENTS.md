## What MatAnyone2Kit does

MatAnyone2Kit is a Swift package for separating a foreground subject from video frames with the MatAnyone2 model on Apple hardware.
It bundles compiled Core ML models; their separate weight license limits use to non-commercial purposes.
Evidence: [README.md](README.md#license); upstream speed claims are not a new benchmark of this fork.
